The app uses a modified version. Changes: conversion from USDZ to local web
geometry and WebP maps; uniform stature normalization; retargeting to exercise
joints; native-weight finger poses; separate rigid hair rendering and bounds;
removal of duplicate eye shells; a light blue top tint and darker fringe tint;
physical materials and exercise-muscle color guidance.

The original facial identity, UV layout and dense hair topology are retained.
