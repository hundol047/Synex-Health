## Description EN
Synex Health helps you manage body-composition and exercise records. Enter measurements, explore illustrative body changes, and build routines based on your goals and experience. Follow clothed avatar demonstrations, log activity and review progress. Device health access and institution sharing are optional. Automatic measurement imports require a supported institution.

The 3D model is not a body scan. Exercise guidance and camera estimation are not medical assessment. See the app for Free and Plus availability; when Plus is enabled, the store displays price and renewal period.
